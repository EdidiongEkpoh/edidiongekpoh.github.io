NYC 311 Service Requests

Updates daily

What drives response time and complaint volume across New York City?

An analysis of NYC's 311 requests, from noise complaints to broken heat, looking at whether response speed varies by borough and community board, and which volume patterns are seasonal versus genuine trends.

  1. SourceNYC Open Data (Socrata)
  2. IngestPython
  3. StorePartitioned Parquet
  4. Modeldbt + DuckDB
  5. ServeEvidence on GitHub Pages, Tableau

Scheduled and tested by GitHub Actions: daily ingestion and dbt build on every push

  • Monthly checkpoints to eliminate large backfills.
  • Tests split by severity. Duplicate or null keys fail the build, like close dates before open dates, raise warnings.
  • The dashboard only after the data passes dbt build/tests. A freshness check confirms the daily job is still running.

Hoop Analytics

Refreshes daily

How do NBA teams and players really compare, once you control for pace and playing time?

A four-page NBA dashboard covering league overview, team profiles, player profiles, and head-to-head comparisons across seasons, backed by a containerized warehouse that refreshes every day.

  1. Sourcenba_api
  2. IngestPython, pandas, SQLAlchemy
  3. StorePostgreSQL
  4. Modeldbt
  5. ServeStreamlit

Orchestrated by Apache Airflow, all services run in Docker Compose

  • Includes player shart charts, plus shot-type splits for catch-and-shoot, pull-ups, drives, and post-ups.
  • Every stat line switches between per game, per 36 minutes, and per 75/100 possessions, with percentile badges against respective season/league.
  • Team profiles break down the Four Factors on offense and defense with league-rank badges and a rolling net rating visual.

Sports YouTube Analytics

Manual refresh

What publishing patterns and content choices drive views and engagement on niche sports channels?

A study of five sports analysis channels, including Thinking Basketball and Hoop Intellect, comparing upload timing, video length, and title style against views and engagement.

  1. SourceYouTube Data API v3
  2. IngestPython extraction
  3. TransformPython, TextBlob
  4. StoreBigQuery + Google Drive
  5. ServeTableau Public
  • Engineered title features such as sentiment, word count, and whether a title asks a question, uses a number, or frames a "vs" matchup.
  • Kept raw pulls timestamped and append-only, separate from rebuilt "current state" tables, so every snapshot stays reproducible.
  • Worked around Tableau Public's connector limits by exporting curated data to Google Drive through OAuth.
